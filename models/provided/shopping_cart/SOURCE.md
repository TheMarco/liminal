# Shopping cart

- **Title:** `Shopping cart LOD`
- **Creator:** Supplied directly by the project owner on 2026-09-17 (source
  file `shopping_cart_lod.glb`).
- **License:** Project-owned asset supplied for use as embedded game content.
- **Local file:** `shopping_cart.glb`
- **Authored specification:** Metres, Y=0 at the floor. A 0.66 m wide cart,
  1.15 m at the handle and 1.19 m long, with basket, child seat, lower rack
  and four casters; the handle sits on local +Z, matching the placement
  pivot, and the footprint centre is 18 mm forward of the origin.
- **Modifications:** The supplied static GLB is retained unchanged. Runtime
  uses it at 88% scale for a 1.01m handle, recentered and given a
  whole-cart collider. Loaded grocery contents move inward to stay inside
  the narrower basket.
