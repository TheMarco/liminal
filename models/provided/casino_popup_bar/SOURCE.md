- **Authored specification:** Metres, Y=0 at the floor. A 3.26 m wide
  counter unit with backbar display, brass dome lamp and three stools on

  lights ride with the unit: dome lamp, two display spots, one votive glow.
- **Modifications:** The supplied static GLB is retained unchanged. Runtime
  uses it at native scale, placed only in slot rooms and the smaller
  table-game rooms the big bar skips, at most one per room against a
  qualifying wall with door and overlap clearance. One collider covers the
  whole unit including the stools. The authored lights are tuned down and
  tagged with their visible fixtures per project convention.
