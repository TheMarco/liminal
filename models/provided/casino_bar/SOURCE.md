- **Title:** `Casino bar`

- **Authored specification:** Metres, Y=0 at the floor. A 6.00 m wide back
  bar with front counter, stocked bottle shelves, brass lamps and an arched
  CASINO BAR sign at 3.34 m; five stools face the counter on the +Z front
  and the rear sits 1.77 m behind the pivot. Packed base-color/normal/label
  texture set extracted at import. Twelve short-range shadowless accent
  lights ride with the unit: undercounter LEDs, shelf accents and two warm
  counter lamps.
- **Modifications:** The supplied static GLB is retained unchanged. Runtime
  uses it at native scale, placed only against backing walls at least as
  tall as the sign in grand halls and ballrooms, with door and overlap
  clearance from the shared wall-bay search. One collider covers the whole
  unit including the stools. The authored lights are tagged with their
  visible fixtures per project convention.
