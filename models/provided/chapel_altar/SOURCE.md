  file `small_church_altar.glb`).

- **Local file:** `chapel_altar.glb`
- **Authored specification:** Metres, Y=0 at the floor. A dressed oak altar,

  on reading stand, candlesticks with emissive flames, linen and paten;
  the dressed front faces local +Z. Thirty-one meshes at 29,808 triangles
  with packed PNG textures extracted at import. Small emissive candle
  flames, no other authored lights.
- **Modifications:** The supplied static GLB is retained unchanged. Runtime
  uses it at native scale, centred, on the chapel dais facing the pews.
