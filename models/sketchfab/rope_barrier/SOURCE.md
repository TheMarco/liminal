- **License:** [Sketchfab Standard](https://sketchfab.com/licenses)
- **Local file:** `rope_barrier.glb`
- **Modifications:** Supplied by the project owner and used as embedded game content. Placed at
  0.62184 scale for a 2.23 x 1.02 x 0.34 m unit — two stanchions 1.891 m
  apart with the swag between them. Casino and cinema queue lines are laid
  out on that pitch rather than the model being stretched to a chosen one,
  which would take the post height with it. Only the posts collide, so a
  player can duck a queue line the way the geometry suggests. Textures re-
  encoded at 1024 px.
