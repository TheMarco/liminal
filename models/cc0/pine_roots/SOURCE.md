# Pine Roots

- **Title:** [Pine Roots](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_roots)
- **Creator:** Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven
- **License:** [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license)
- **Files used:** 1K glTF, shared geometry buffer, and the two included 1K PBR
  material sets (diffuse, OpenGL normal, and ARM).
- **Use:** Rare hero root masses in the Bloom's atria and heart chambers.
- **Modifications:** No source-file geometry edits. Godot imports the glTF and
  runtime placement changes scale, orientation, and lighting context.
