# mall_photo_booth

Original reference-led orange-curtain photo booth; aluminum extrusion frame, woodgrain panels, real interior with checker floor and bench, folded cloth mesh, camera and pickup slots.

Authored in Blender with `tools/blender/build_mall_nostalgia.py`. Editable source and front/reverse previews are in `art/mall_photo_booth`. No purchased or downloaded meshes. Reference photographs supplied by the user guide proportions and design.

Game axes: +Y up, +Z front, floor at Y=0; metres. Shared 1024px PBR atlas. Triangle ceiling: 4000; exact counts and bounds in `mesh_stats.json`. Booth original signage uses one additional image surface.
