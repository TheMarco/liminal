1m wide, 1.95m tall. Faces +Z. Shell .46m deep; handle brings total depth to .58m.

- Runtime: `school_cupboard.glb` with packed PBR textures
- Export: 552 triangles, 1 material surfaces
- Finite vertex attributes, triangle degeneracy and budgets checked by `tools/audit_authored_hero_meshes.py`.
