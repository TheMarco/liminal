# mall_rocket_ride

Original reference-led cobalt/red fiberglass rocket kiddie ride; custom open-cockpit fuselage, molded red seat, swept wings, T-tail, raised payment pedestal and rubber bellows mounting.

Authored in Blender with `tools/blender/build_mall_nostalgia.py`. Editable source and front/reverse previews are in `art/mall_rocket_ride`. No purchased or downloaded meshes. Reference photographs supplied by the user guide proportions and design.

Game axes: +Y up, +Z front, floor at Y=0; metres. Shared 1024px PBR atlas. Triangle ceiling: 4500; exact counts and bounds in `mesh_stats.json`. Booth original signage uses one additional image surface.

Windshield, dark surround, and nose paint are flush source artwork (`art/mall_rocket_ride/draw_paint.py`), baked directly into the one-surface hull atlas. Rounded tail endcaps and smoothed cockpit rim. Final mesh: 3,290 triangles.
