## Modifications

Uniformly scaled to a 1.01m handle height, floor-centred and rotated 180 degrees
inside the generated placement pivot so the authored handle matches the former
cart's facing convention. It replaces every generated shopping cart in the
mall. Existing deterministic cart contents are retained for loaded variants,
and loose carts receive one conservative gameplay collider. The source mesh and
materials are otherwise unchanged.
