- **Modifications:** Desk and chair are modelled as one unit, so it replaces
  both halves of a generated desk-and-seat pair rather than sitting alongside
  one. The source root's baked 136.9088° presentation turn is removed so the
  chair-to-desk axis follows the generated classroom facing. Scaled to 0.007,
  re-origined to its own footprint centre and floor, given conservative
  collision, and spaced from its corrected axis-aligned footprint. Used as the
  canonical student station throughout classroom rows. Embedded textures
  re-encoded at 1024 px; mesh and material unchanged.
