- **Modifications:** Uniformly scaled to `0.72`, floor-centred from measured
  imported bounds, and used as the central island in every school chemistry
  lab. This places its worktop at a standard `0.86m` while preserving doorway
  circulation. The physically incorrect metallic granite is converted to a rough
  dielectric black worktop, and the nickel roughness is raised so both remain
  legible beneath the generated fluorescent lighting. Geometry and the
  embedded nickel texture are otherwise unchanged.
