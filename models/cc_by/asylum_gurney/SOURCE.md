# Game Prop: Mental Asylum Gurney

- **Creator:** [Ellie](https://sketchfab.com/eymori)
- **Source:** <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/game-prop-mental-asylum-gurney-43736bb6a0b64fe8a824542bdbace6bc>
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Local file:** `asylum_gurney.glb`
- **Modifications:** Placed at authored scale (1.88 × 1.21 × 0.76 m) and given
  conservative collision. Its `Chair_mechanics` material ships a
  metallic-roughness map pinning roughness to zero, which renders as a black
  cut-out under the game's dim practicals; a roughness floor is applied once to
  that shared material when the scene first loads. Albedo, normal maps, mesh and
  the remaining three materials are unchanged. Embedded textures re-encoded at
  1024 px.
