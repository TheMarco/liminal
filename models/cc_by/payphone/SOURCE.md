# Payphone

- **Creator:** [mtaesiri](https://sketchfab.com/mtaesiri)
- **Source:** <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/payphone-ec3a176820074776a3632c6feb0b8327>
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Local file:** `payphone.glb`
- **Modifications:** The source is a photogrammetry scan of a payphone together
  with the tiled wall behind it — 88,025 triangles, of which the wall is most.
  The mesh is clipped to the phone housing alone (0.30 × 0.62 × 0.13 m,
  19,716 triangles) and re-origined so its open back sits on the wall contact
  plane, leaving placement code to supply only a point and a yaw. Its single
  material and baked texture are unchanged apart from a 1024 px re-encode.
