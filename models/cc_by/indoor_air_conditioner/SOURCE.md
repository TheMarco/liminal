- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Local file:** `indoor_air_conditioner_unit.glb`
- **Modifications:** Uniformly scaled to `2.35`, producing an approximately
  `1.25 × 0.42 × 0.31 m` indoor unit, and re-centred from measured imported
  bounds. One or two instances mount high on deterministic office-room walls;
  corridors are excluded. Solid undecorated walls are preferred, doorway
  headers are a high-clearance fallback, and rare wall-less open-plan rooms use
  a generated ceiling-suspended backing plate. No gameplay collision is added.
  The source mesh and embedded PBR materials are otherwise unchanged.
