## Modifications

The supplied GLB is one approximately 81-metre material-merged environment,
not a modular kit. One complete double exit-door assembly was isolated by
world-space triangle position, including its frame, two leaves, handles,
translucent EXIT sign face and sign housing. It was rebased to a centred,
floor-level origin and saved as a standalone binary Godot scene. The opaque
door and frame materials were changed from presentation-scene unshaded mode to
per-pixel lighting so they respond to the Annex fluorescents; the sign
materials retain their authored presentation treatment. No surrounding level
geometry is included.

The extracted door appears as an occasional sealed Annex exit facade. It is
not presented as a separately supplied source asset.
