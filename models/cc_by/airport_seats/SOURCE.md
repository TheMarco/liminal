- **Modifications:** Uniformly scaled to `0.04`, floor-centred from measured
  imported bounds, and rotated so its four seats face the same direction as
  the rows they replace. Source camera/light staging nodes are removed at
  runtime. A conservative `2.10 × 0.83 × 0.62 m` collider is bound to each
  complete seat bank.
