## Modifications

The supplied staged set is separated at runtime into its three authored pieces:
a backpack and two rolling cases. Each piece is floor-centred, uniformly scaled
to real-world carry-on size, and receives a conservative gameplay collider when
placed loose. Deterministic muted color variants are applied only to the
authored shell and fabric materials; handles, wheels, zippers and metal hardware
retain their source materials. The same pieces replace the former generated
luggage on airport floors, trolleys and baggage carousels.
