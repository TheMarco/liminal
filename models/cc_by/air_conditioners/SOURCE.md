- **Modifications:** The original collection is retained. Its three complete floor condenser units are spatially extracted into `air_conditioner_floor_a.scn`, `air_conditioner_floor_b.scn`, and `air_conditioner_floor_c.scn` by `tools/extract_air_conditioners.gd`, with the source parent rotation baked and each unit centred and bottom-aligned for individual placement. Geometry, UVs, colour/alpha and normal maps are retained; the legacy specular/gloss workflow is converted to a lightweight engine-native painted-metal material shared by the three scenes. Godot-extracted embedded textures remain adjacent to the local file.
