# Pool ladder

- **Source:** <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ladder-pool-3ad9ae8935b74a71a9aff4c6f199f658>
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Local file:** `pool_ladder.glb`
- **Modifications:** Scaled to the Poolrooms' 1.42m freeboard and stood on the
  basin floor at every water-to-dry edge, so its rungs run from under the
  surface up to the lip and its grab rails rise over the deck. The climbable
  volume is a separate Area3D built alongside it in `Chunk._pool_ladder_at`;
  the model itself is unaltered.
