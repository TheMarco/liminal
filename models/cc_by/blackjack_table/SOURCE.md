# Blackjack table

- **Creator:** [nermin](https://sketchfab.com/nermin)
- **Source:** <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/blackjack-table-b2b0974f06ee4623b882c76378a59483>
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Local file:** `blackjack_table.glb`
- **Modifications:** The source is 500,000 triangles, 84% of which is a `Chips`
  group — 40 casino chips modelled at 10,500 triangles each. That group is
  removed, leaving the table, felt and six matching stools at 80,000 triangles
  and taking the file from 17.9 MB to 4.4 MB. The remainder is re-origined to
  its own floor and centre, placed at 0.00407 for a 3.46 x 1.10 x 1.76 m
  setpiece, and given collision on the table body only so the player can still
  walk between the stools. Embedded textures re-encoded at 1024 px; the kept
  meshes and materials are unchanged.
