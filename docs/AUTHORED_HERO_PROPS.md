## Behavior and placement

- Carousel belt and luggage follow the oval at the same speed; the shell does
  not rotate. Its footprint is 7 × 3m with a 0.665m belt height; luggage and
  screens keep their human scale. Its arrivals screen and mounting posts face the long side (+X).
  Number backing, screen, feed hood and belt belong to one removable furnishing.
- ECT and restraint models replace treatment-room primitives, including colliders
  sized to the open case lid and rolling bed. There are no duplicate old bodies.
- VT100 retains live screen shader, query/next/reset interaction and readout.
  Monitor and keyboard sit on the measured 0.7875m guard-desk top. Both remain
  children of the same workstation as their supporting desk.
- Kiosks become open U-shaped retail stations. Their register faces inward (-X)
  toward staff. Collision leaves the staff well and rear entry open. Smaller
  display-table placements use the compact shelving companion.
- Incubators keep room placement/scale variations, with a rigid shell, clear
  glass, cloudy fluid, slowly rising bubbles and a pulsing egg wholly in liquid.

## Rebuild and verification

Blender builders live in `tools/blender/build_airport_carousel.py`,
`build_asylum_medical.py`, `build_vt100.py`, `build_mall_merchandise.py`, and
`build_bloom_incubator.py`. Run `draw_hero_labels.py` using Python first for the
shared original signage, then each builder with Blender background mode.

`python3 tools/audit_authored_hero_meshes.py` reads the real GLBs and checks
triangle/surface budgets, embedded textures, finite attributes and zero-area
triangles. `audit_authored_hero_props.gd` exercises the terminal callbacks,
carousel continuity/tangent headings/stopped state, egg containment throughout
its pulse, rigid glass and preload registration. Existing airport luggage,
Bloom, doorway, overlap and constructor audits check generated placement.

Native studio captures are generated by `capture_procedural_props.gd` (its
`--filter` accepts comma-separated names). `capture_authored_hero_rooms.gd`
captures all five affected floors in the actual game scene, with production
lighting and optional tape processing, under `build/hero-prop-review/rooms`.
Run `python3 tools/build_hero_prop_gallery.py` after captures to refresh the
focused model gallery and the matching after images in the older gallery.

Native inspection corrected atlas mipmap bleed on the CRT bevels, a secondary
UV stream on the ECT instrument panel, and the keyboard's old buried placement.
The user-requested screen orientations are included in the final assets.

For the initial six-model pass, the 522-chunk fingerprint was regenerated and reproduced identically
on a second run. Twenty-six changed chunks contain the replacements. One school
library chunk also changes only book colors by small RGB amounts: the existing
`Mats.sch_chair` cache rounds its hue key to two decimals but creates the color
from the first caller's full hue. Removing earlier primitive furnishings changes
that first caller. Its complete 320-node structure, transforms and geometry were
compared with the prior detail dump and remain identical.

The subsequent gate desk is 3,904 triangles across two shared surfaces, with
agent-facing screens and nine grouped colliders. Its four-orientation runtime
audit and existing airport-collider / 1,406-room overlap checks pass. Only three
airport gate entries change in the 522-chunk fingerprint for this addition.

## Airport moving walkway — September 7

The reference-based walkway replaces the procedural transit and concourse units.
Both Blender exports contain **3,576 triangles / three surfaces**. Native 8.4m
and 10.4m units share the same 1.15m belt width and 13cm deck height; a cached mesh
fit adjusts only the straight middle for the concourse's 9.4m length. Rounded
newels, rubber rail cross-sections and entry ramps retain their dimensions.

The named `WalkwayBelt` mesh uses the existing animated pallet shader at signed
0.75m/s. The existing Travelator area still carries the player in each lane's
travel direction. Five simple colliders now follow the authored deck and entry
ramps. Clear glazing is enclosed by visible black handrails and steel housings;
the airport barrier audit checks that opaque frame instead of requiring a heavy
tint on a framed pane.

Source: `tools/blender/build_airport_walkway.py`, editable scenes and renders in
`art/airport_walkway/`, runtime exports in `models/authored/airport_walkway/`.
The hero gallery includes both Blender angles, native Godot previews, and actual
airport views with and without the tape effect.

Validation: mesh budgets/degeneracy; five lengths × two orientations × two belt
directions; real CharacterBody3D carry in both directions; 1,406 generated rooms
without furnishing overlaps; airport collider coverage; world digest changes
restricted to airport records.

dispensers have no freestanding fallback. Only explicitly tagged architecture
can support their backs; corridor door panels and headers cannot substitute for
full-height walls. Rooms without suitable clear wall space omit the fixture.

asset, placement, backing and shared-resource assertions; exported meshes checked
for finite attributes, degenerate triangles, embedded maps and budgets; room
screenshots reviewed. Existing general prop-overlap sweep also passed.

frame or machine. These are fictional period graphics, not reference-photo crops.
