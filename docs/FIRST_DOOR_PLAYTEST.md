# First doorway blind playtest

Use this sheet to test whether a new player discovers the camera doorway in
the first ten minutes without being taught the mechanic. Run an ordinary new
Descent session. Record the build and seed. Start the play timer when control
is handed to the player; record loading and intro duration separately. Do not explain that the camera can
show another room or that a photograph can change the wall. Give only the
standard controls if the player asks how to operate the game. Stop at ten
minutes or when the first tape is reached, whichever comes first.

During play, do not prompt or correct the player. Record the time of the first
detector noticed, camera raised, doorway seen, shutter taken, and doorway
entered. Also record whether the player spontaneously scans another wall after
the first discovery and whether assistance was requested. Use “not observed”
when the behavior did not happen during the session; use “failure” only for a
technical or otherwise unintended problem.

After play, ask neutrally: “What do you think happened, and what would you try
next?” Write the participant’s explanation in their own words. Do not prime
them during play or replace their explanation with the intended rule.

Treat “discover and open” as both seeing the doorway through the camera and
entering it within ten minutes without an explanation of the mechanic. The
initial directional target is at least **4 of 5** participants. A second target
is at least **3 of 5** spontaneously rescanning another wall after the first
discovery. Record exact counts and notable confusion even if the targets are
missed. Five sessions provide directional product feedback, not a prediction
of Steam performance.
