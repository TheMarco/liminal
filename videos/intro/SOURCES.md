  added 2026-09-29. The five-second studio logo plays once per normal launch,
  then crossfades immediately into the prepared title. Converted to
  Theora/Vorbis at 1920×1080, 24 fps
  (video quality 9, audio quality 6), retaining the original audio and aspect.

  1920×1080 H.264/AAC, to 1280×720) up to 1:01.1, then Hedra Seedance 2.5
  clips and an engine render of the game's casino and elevator, edited by
  `tools/intro_assemble.py`, then edited by the owner (exported as
  `deliverables/intro-desert-oasis/intro.mp4.mp4`, 1920×1080) and encoded at
  1280×720, 25 fps (ffmpeg2theora vq 7, aq 6). Production record:
  `docs/INTRO_CUTSCENE_SCRIPT.md`.
