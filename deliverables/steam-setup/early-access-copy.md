## why

It Wants You To Stay is a playable horror game that is still taking shape. Early Access gives supporters a way to play, explore its unsettling spaces, and help shape the experience through feedback.

We want to learn where the tension works, where exploration becomes confusing, and how encounters and progression feel to different players. That feedback will help guide gameplay decisions, balancing, usability, and development priorities.

## how_long

No fixed release date has been set for version 1.0. The length of Early Access will depend on what we learn from playtesting and the work needed to refine the experience. We plan to share progress and changes to our plans through Steam announcements.

## full_version

We plan to use Early Access feedback to refine exploration, encounter design, pacing, and progression, alongside improvements to usability, performance, and overall polish. Our aim is a more cohesive and satisfying horror experience for the full release.

The scope and priorities may change as we learn from players. We are not committing to a fixed feature list or release schedule.

## current_state

The current version is playable and includes the core first-person exploration and horror experience: unsettling environments, entity encounters, photography mechanics, and the game's found-footage presentation. The About This Game section describes the current experience in more detail.

Development is ongoing. Gameplay, pacing, balance, and presentation may change, and players may encounter bugs or performance issues. Early Access is intended for people who want to enjoy the current game and take part in playtesting as it develops.

## pricing

We currently plan to keep the same base price during Early Access and at the full release.

Buying during Early Access gives you ownership of the game, including subsequent game updates and the full version when it is released, without needing to buy it again. There is no separate upgrade charge to move from Early Access to version 1.0.

## community

We invite players to use the Steam Discussions for bug reports, feedback, and suggestions. Tell us which moments stayed with you, where you got stuck, how encounters felt, and what you would like to see improved. For technical issues, details about your system and the steps that led to the problem are especially helpful.

We plan to use this feedback to help prioritize development and share updates through Steam announcements. We cannot implement every suggestion, but player experiences will help shape the game.
