It Wants You To Stay was developed using generative AI tools, including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Meta Muse, Meshy, Scenario, Eleven Labs, Suno AI, ChatGPT, and Seedance. AI was used to create code, many of the 3D models, sound effects, music, and videos. The game also includes free-to-use, human-made 3D models, with credit given to their authors.

I understand that some players feel strongly about AI in games, and I respect that. If you prefer not to support games made using AI, this title may not be for you. I’m sharing this openly so you can make an informed choice before buying.

If you choose to play, I hope you’ll judge the experience it delivers—its atmosphere, gameplay, performance, and value. Honest feedback, including negative reviews, is welcome. I take responsibility for the game’s quality and will use that feedback to help improve it.
