[h1]Update 0.6.4 — Smoother room loading and blackouts[/h1]

[list]
[*]The Office starts preparing furniture earlier as you approach new rooms. The Airport builds gates over several frames and draws ceiling fixtures and glass markings in fewer batches.
[*]Rooms reuse materials and meshes for repeated surfaces. The game spreads out the work of placing optional tapes and skips redundant updates to doorways revealed by the camera.
[*]Blackouts now prepare numbered signs ahead of time and save layout changes together. Shifting hallways calculate their new shape in the background.
[*]Floor music is cued before the screen fades in. Casino slot machines skip unnecessary audio updates, and setting a monster on fire reuses animation data the game has already prepared.
[/list]

If you still feel a pause, please include the floor, the time on the in-game clock, and the log from that session when you report it.
