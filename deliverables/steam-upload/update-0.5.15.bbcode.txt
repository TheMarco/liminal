[h1]Update 0.5.15 — Smoother exploration and fewer pauses[/h1]

This update tackles pauses you could feel when walking up to a doorway, taking a photo, or waiting for the Casino lights to go out.

[list]
[*]Realm doorways render faster as you approach. On an M3 Max, 95% of frames on our doorway walk finished within 15.5 ms, down from 19.7 ms.
[*]Casino blackouts spread room rebuilding across frames. Partly loaded rooms can resume after a power change.
[*]Photos now save in the background, reducing the pause after the shutter.
[*]Settings reopens faster, and the Mall, Poolrooms, and late Annex generate faster.
[/list]

On Mac, the first load into the Casino can still pause for a long time while the game prepares its graphics. We're continuing to investigate that delay.

If you feel a pause in 0.5.15, please tell us what floor you were on, what happened just before it, and which operating system and graphics card you use. Thank you for helping us track these down.
