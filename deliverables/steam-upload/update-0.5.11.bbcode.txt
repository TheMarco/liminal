[h1]0.5.11 — Performance improvements and smoother exploration[/h1]

This update focuses on reducing unnecessary rendering work and the frame-time spikes that can interrupt exploration.

Thank you to everyone who sent logs, screenshots and detailed reports. They helped narrow down several problems, including lights repeatedly redrawing shadows in rooms where nothing was moving.

[h2]What's changed[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]More efficient shadows.[/b] Reworked shadow allocation to improve reuse of existing shadows, especially in rooms with many lights. The Bloom's pulsing growths and the airport's moving luggage also require fewer shadow updates.
[*][b]Lower texture memory requirements.[/b] World, model and painting textures now use GPU compression, mipmaps and a 2K size limit. UI, ghost sprites, portal panoramas and CRT lookup textures keep their existing treatment.
[*][b]Less work when leaving rooms.[/b] Unloaded rooms are now removed gradually across frames instead of deleting a whole group at once.
[*][b]Lighter realm previews.[/b] Looking through a seal no longer runs a second full set of expensive lighting effects. The destination uses its full environment when you enter.
[*][b]Less repeated work between floors.[/b] Shared shaders stay loaded across floor changes. Small decorative objects and some rounded meshes also have a lower rendering cost.
[*][b]Better defaults and diagnostics.[/b] New Windows and Linux settings profiles on integrated graphics start on Low. Existing preferences are preserved. Additional logging will help investigate remaining performance problems.
[/list]

[h2]Visual trade-offs[/h2]
Some lamp shadows may look softer up close, some previously 4K textures are now 2K, and the view through a realm seal may look flatter than the destination itself.

[h2]Windows testing is continuing[/h2]
These changes address specific sources of rendering and streaming cost. They are not a promise that every freeze or crash is fixed; startup stalls and Windows stability remain under investigation.

If you still encounter problems, you can try [code]--rendering-driver vulkan[/code] in Steam's launch options. It has helped some testers, but results vary and the default driver has not changed.

Please include your GPU, graphics settings, whether you used Vulkan, and your latest game and performance logs with further reports. Thank you for helping me make the game run better.
