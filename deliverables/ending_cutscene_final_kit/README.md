## Non-negotiable continuity

- The player character is never visible.
- The warm brown/orange elevator doors begin fully closed and open only halfway.
- The casino cannot appear in the first exterior reveal. It appears only when the camera later looks back.
- The elevator is part of the Desert Oasis entrance, never a freestanding structure.
- The wasteland contains several wrecked cars and sparse, old human remains without fresh gore.
- Generate clean, rectilinear 16:9 footage: no HUD, CRT curvature, barrel distortion, vignette, scanlines, chromatic aberration, or VHS treatment.
- Apply the game's CRT/camcorder shader only after the four clips have been generated and assembled.
